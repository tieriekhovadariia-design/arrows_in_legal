The short version. Arrows In! has no accounts of its own, so there is no account to close. Almost everything the Game knows about you lives on your phone and, on Android, in a cloud copy in your own Google account; you can erase both yourself in a minute. For the small amount of data held by Google services, you can write to us and we will delete what can be linked to your device.

1. Erase the data on your device

Your progress, crowns, settings and stock of hints and tries are stored on your device (and, on Android, in the cloud copy described in section 2). To delete the copy on your device:

  • Android: open Settings → Apps → Arrows In! → Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the Game.
  • iOS: uninstall the Game (press and hold its icon, then Remove App → Delete App). “Offload App” keeps the data, so choose Delete.

This is immediate and permanent on the device. The one-time “No ads” purchase is held by the app store, not by the Game, and comes back with Restore Purchases in the shop if you install the Game again.

2. Erase the cloud copy (Android)

If you were signed in to Google Play Games on Android, the Game also kept a copy of your progress in your Google account, so that it survives a reinstall. Uninstalling the Game does not delete it. To delete it:

  • open Google Drive settings on a computer, choose Manage apps, find Arrows In!, open Options and choose Delete hidden app data; or
  • delete the Game’s data, or your whole Google Play Games profile, in the settings of the Google Play Games app.

We have no copy of your progress of our own, so once you delete it there, it is gone. To stop the Game from making a new copy, sign out of Google Play Games or turn off automatic sign-in in the Google Play Games app.

3. Ask us to delete the data held by Google services

The Game uses Google AdMob for ads, Google Firebase for usage statistics and crash reports, and RevenueCat to verify purchases. They work with random identifiers (and, for RevenueCat on Android, your Google Play Games player ID), not with your name or email. To ask for that data to be deleted:

  1. Write to tieriekhova.dariia@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data — Arrows In!”.
  2. Tell us your device model and roughly when you last played. You do not need to send anything else, and we will never ask for a password or payment details.
  3. We reply within 30 days. We will delete, through Google’s deletion tools and in our own copy of the statistics, the records we can link to your device, and tell you honestly what we could and could not find. If you can find your Firebase app instance identifier, include it: it is the one thing that lets us find your statistics records reliably.

Because there are no accounts, we usually cannot identify a particular player in Google’s data, and we will not collect extra information just to search for you. These steps always work on their own:

  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID — on Android in Settings → Privacy → Ads, on iOS by turning off Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking for the Game. This breaks the link between past ad data and your device.
  • Future collection: in regions where the Game asks for consent, withdraw it in Settings → Privacy Settings inside the Game.

4. What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Progress, crowns, settings, hints and tries on your deviceDeleted by you, immediately and permanently (section 1).
Cloud copy of your progress in your Google account (Android)Deleted by you in Google Drive or Google Play Games (section 2).
Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics, its BigQuery export and our own copy of that export)Deleted on request where we can link them to your device; otherwise deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)Deleted on request where we can link them to your device; otherwise deleted automatically after at most 90 days.
Advertising data (Google AdMob)Held by Google under its own policy; resetting your advertising ID unlinks it from your device. Manage it at myadcenter.google.com.
Purchase recordsKept by Apple or Google as their terms and tax law require. The copy held for us by RevenueCat, linked to a random installation identifier or your Google Play Games player ID, is deleted on request where tax and accounting law allow.
Your email to usKept for up to two years, then deleted. Tell us if you want it deleted sooner.
