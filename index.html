Frequently asked

I reinstalled the Game and my progress is gone.

Progress is stored on the device only; there is no cloud save yet, so a reinstall or a new device starts from the beginning. One-time purchases — “No ads” and board themes — come back with Settings → Restore purchases on the same store account. Unused hints and undo refills cannot be restored, because they are consumable items already delivered.

I paid but I do not see the item.

Use Settings → Restore purchases first. If it still does not appear, send us the store receipt and we will look into it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or your order history on Google Play. If the problem is our fault, tell us and we will back up your request.

How do I change my advertising or privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy options in the Game to reopen the consent dialog, or use your device settings. The details are in section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

A level looks impossible.

Every level in the Game has at least one solution, and the hint will tell you when a position can no longer be solved — restart it and the same set of arrows will work in a different order. If you believe a level is genuinely unsolvable, send us the level number and a screenshot; that is exactly the kind of report we want.
